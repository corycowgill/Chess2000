The Pieces

Six Chicago landmarks

Willis Tower

King

Bruce Graham's 1973 bundled-tube skyscraper with stepped setbacks and twin antennae.

John Hancock Center

Queen

1969 trapezoidal tower with diagonal X-bracing on every face.

Marina City

Rook

Bertrand Goldberg's 1964 cylindrical “corncob” tower with scalloped balconies.

Cloud Gate

Bishop

Anish Kapoor's mirrored sculpture — “The Bean” — in Millennium Park.

Chicago Theatre

Knight

The 1921 vertical CHICAGO sign and arched marquee over State Street.

Flag Star

Pawn

The six-pointed red star — one of four on the Chicago flag.
